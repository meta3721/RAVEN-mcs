\begin{proposition}[Practical plug-in perturbation]
\label{prop:practical_plugin}
Assume A1, A4, and the quantitative positivity A5 of the G4 note
(target-supported margins $\pi_{\min}$, $p_{\min}^{\mathrm{true}}$,
$q_{\min}^{\mathrm{true}}$; uniform plug-in errors
$\varepsilon_\pi,\varepsilon_p,\varepsilon_q$; clipping residual
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{clip}}$; H\'ajek denominator floor $B_{\min}$;
and a common active set $\mathcal A_r$, composition $M_r$, and feasible
set $\mathcal C_r$).
Let $\boldsymbol\beta_r^\star$ be the same-window ideal untruncated
reference and $\widehat{\boldsymbol\beta}_r$ the implemented clipped
plug-in.  Let $\boldsymbol\alpha^\star(\boldsymbol\beta)$ denote the unique
P2 minimizer at that fixed window state.  Then
\begin{align}
\bigl\|\widehat{\boldsymbol\beta}_r-\boldsymbol\beta_r^\star\bigr\|_1
&\le
C_1\bigl(\varepsilon_\pi+\varepsilon_p+\varepsilon_q+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{clip}}\bigr),
\label{eq:g4_beta}\\
\bigl\|\widehat{\boldsymbol\alpha}_r-\boldsymbol\alpha_r^\star\bigr\|_2
&\le
\bigl\|\widehat{\boldsymbol\beta}_r-\boldsymbol\beta_r^\star\bigr\|_2,
\label{eq:g4_alpha}\\
\bigl\|M_r(\widehat{\boldsymbol\alpha}_r-\boldsymbol\alpha_r^\star)\bigr\|_2
&\le
\|M_r\|_2\,
\bigl\|\widehat{\boldsymbol\beta}_r-\boldsymbol\beta_r^\star\bigr\|_2,
\label{eq:g4_comp}
\end{align}
where
\begin{equation}
C_1
=
\frac{2K_r n_r}{B_{\min}}
\max\Biggl\{
\frac{d_{\max}}{\pi_{\min}^2 p_{\min}},\;
\frac{d_{\max}}{\pi_{\min}p_{\min}p_{\min}^{\mathrm{true}}},\;
\frac{a_{\max}}{q_{\min}q_{\min}^{\mathrm{true}}},\;
a_{\max}+d_{\max}
\Biggr\}.
\label{eq:g4_c1}
\end{equation}
The result is a stability bound.  It does not assert unbiasedness of the
practical estimator, identification on $\{\pi^{\mathrm{tar}}=0\}$, or
robustness to a change of $\mathcal A_r$.
\end{proposition}

\noindent\emph{Proof sketch.}
Decompose $a-\widetilde a= -(\widetilde a-a_{\max})_+$ and likewise for
$d$; these residuals are $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{clip}}$ and are not
neglected.  On target support the unclipped maps
$(\widehat\zeta,\widehat p)\mapsto\widetilde a$ and
$\widehat q\mapsto\widetilde d$ are Lipschitz with the displayed
margins.  The unnormalized masses $b=md$ therefore move by at most
$K_r n_r$ times a linear form in
$(\varepsilon_\pi,\varepsilon_p,\varepsilon_q,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{clip}})$.
The H\'ajek map $b\mapsto b/(\mathbf 1^\top b)$ is $2/B_{\min}$-Lipschitz
in $\ell_1$ on $\{b\ge0:\mathbf 1^\top b\ge B_{\min}\}$.
The P2 map $\boldsymbol\beta\mapsto\boldsymbol\alpha^\star(\boldsymbol\beta)$
is non-expansive in $\ell_2$ on a fixed convex $\mathcal C_r$
(appendix reference-stability argument).
\hfill$\square$
