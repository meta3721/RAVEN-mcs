APPENDIXK
LEARNINGCAPACITY UNDER ASEPARATEDEVELOPMENT
PROTOCOL
These measurements use chronological development val-
idation splits and seeds 41001–41003, rather than the test
comparisons in the main paper. Central-delivered pools only
delivered training observations. Both backbones are evaluated
over 200 effective data passes, with Adam learning rates
.0003, .001, .003and momentum-SGD rates.001, .01, .03,
batch size 64, and a factor-.3 schedule after passes 50,
100, and 150. Configurations and checkpoints are selected
on validation. HistoryResidual adds a 64/32 history network
and a training-statistic residual reference to Common-NDMF.
The 108 central runs, one Traffic Uniform development run,
and three U-Air Central-all diagnostics are separate learning
measurements, not new RA VEN comparisons.
Training means, spatial means, seasonal means, persistence,
and repeated-phase daily/weekly seasonal-naive predictors
share the delivered-training information boundary. Persistence
uses the latest legal training observation; seasonal naive repeats
the latest available phase without feeding validation labels
back into history. Weekly comparison requires at least two
weeks of training observations. Prediction is inverse-scaled
and scored against float64 physical targets. Traffic’s target
is traffic volume; the other two retain their controlled client-
measurement definitions.
Common-NDMF improves on the strongest simple ref-
erence for Traffic and SensorScope but not U-Air. Histo-
ryResidual gives7.33%on Traffic and−0.34%/−4.45%on
SensorScope/U-Air. U-Air Central-all, with all training labels
visible, averages19.5427versus18.4567for its spatial-mean
reference. This distinguishes backbone/generalization limits
from delivery sparsity alone. The single Traffic Uniform run
reaches253.1132vehicles/h (33.70%improvement), but its
best validation error still improves0.914%between passes
100 and 200. The central runs plateau under the declared
criterion, whereas this federated run does not. Neither the
pooled learning gain nor that single development trajectory
establishes a RA VEN weighting benefit.
