2 Discussion of state by state performance 
Uncorrected PurpleAir sensors in this work overestimate PM2.5 in every state on average except for Florida 
(Figure S4); this typical overprediction is indicated by MBE greater than 0 µg m -3. Figure S4 shows the state-by-
state performance before and after correction. The MBE after correction is within 3 µg m-3 in each state with 24-hr 
RMSE ≤ 3.7 µg m-3, showing large improvements from the initial dataset. The RMSE is reduced and the MBE 
moves closer to zero across all states except Alaska and Arizona where R MSE improves but MBE moves slightly 
further from 0 (AK MBEraw=0.8 µg m-3, MBEcorrected=-1.0 µg m-3; AZ MBEraw=0.4 µg m-3, MBEcorrected=-0.5 µg m-3) 
and Florida where correction slightly increases RMSE and increases underprediction (MBE raw=-0.3 µg m-3, 
MBEcorrected=-3.7 µg m-3). It is important to note that the reported performance may not accurately summarize state-
wide performance in states with less than a year of data (indicated in Figure S4 by states with white background 
labels) and in states with a single PurpleAir collocated at a single site. To illustrate, Florida is represented by a 
single PurpleAir sensor located at a single site for less than a year; the performance in Florida may be influenced by 
a different aerosol composition in Sarasota, Florida (e.g., higher sea salt contribution) or may be an issue unique to 
the FEM and PurpleAir running at the site.
