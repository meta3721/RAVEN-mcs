The two selection margins are protocol quantities rather than per-unit estimates. Over all 28800 records the observation rate takes only the two block-level values 0.1925 and 0.35, so it is constant within every opportunity stratum and contributes no within-stratum variation. The usable-update rate is drawn once per client-window and shared by the at most four risk units of that window, so it reweights client-windows without tilting within a risk set. Neither margin varies at the unit level or with the label on these traces.

Both construction parameters are constant within a stratum rather than varying per unit: the observation rate depends only on the UTC block, which the opportunity stratum already fixes, and the usable-update rate is drawn once per client-window and shared by every risk unit in it. The reported predictive gains describe this construction, not the range attainable when selection varies at the unit level.
