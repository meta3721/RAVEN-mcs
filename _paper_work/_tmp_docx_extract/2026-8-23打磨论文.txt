一.作图
(1)，让cursor基于论文模板和跑出来的实验结果。做出我们需要填充的图像的草图。并提供对应数据集。可以让AI多次查几遍，要求用真实数据集，严谨，符合逻辑

(3)，优化这些图像，我整理了老师的一些意见:
1，柱状图色彩不要太鲜艳，颜色里面用条纹填充。柱子整体低一些，上面就有空间放字(比如图例)了

5，图像要紧凑，尽可能把空隙减小。就是整体上紧凑，匀称，协调，美观
6，只有一个图的，就把图例放到方框内。
7，图不能压字。字不压线。
(4)图大致修好之后再仔细检查有哪些不符合要求的或者不美观的地方就再指出图名，发布指令，进行修改。

5： 控制篇幅的原则与方法
总的原则：以欲投稿的目标期刊的篇幅要求为目标控制论文的篇幅。

\appendix
This appendix provides formal theoretical guarantees for the VNPP scheme, including verifiable perturbation correctness, noise-free truth estimation correctness, and convergence properties of user reputation and source weights.
\subsection{Verifiable Perturbation Correctness}
\begin{theorem}[Verifiable Perturbation Correctness]
\label{thm:perturbation}
Let $d_s$ be the raw data of user $w^s$ and $S_s$ be the noise generated by the disturbance server (DS). Let $D_s$ be the perturbed data obtained by DR after homomorphic addition and proxy re-encryption. Then VNPP ensures
\[
\textcolor{black}{D_s - d_s = S_s},
\]
and no party other than $w^s$ or DR can \textcolor{black}{obtain} $d_s$ or $S_s$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each user encrypts its raw data as
\[
\textcolor{black}{d_s' = \mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(d_s)},
\]
while the disturbance server generates the perturbation noise and encrypts
\[
\textcolor{black}{S_s' = \mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(S_s)}.
\]
The blockchain executes homomorphic addition
\[
\textcolor{black}{D_s = d_s' \circledast S_s' = \mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(d_s + S_s)},
\]
which, by the additive homomorphic property, guarantees
\[
\textcolor{black}{\mathrm{Dec}_{sk_s}(D_s) = d_s + S_s}.
\]
Through proxy re-encryption using $\textcolor{black}{rk_s}$, the data requester recovers
\[
\textcolor{black}{D_s = d_s + S_s},
\]
without exposing $d_s$ or $S_s$ to any intermediate party. Since only $w^s$ possesses $\textcolor{black}{sk_s}$ and only DR possesses $\textcolor{black}{sk_\gamma}$, the perturbation is both verifiable and privacy-preserving.
\end{proof}
\subsection{Noise-Free Estimated True Value Correctness}
\begin{lemma}[Noise-Free ETV Correctness]
\label{lem:noise_free_etv}
Given raw data $\{d_s\}_{s=1}^k$ and weights $\{w_s\}$, the VNPP protocol computes a noise-free Estimated True Value
\[
\textcolor{black}{X = \frac{\sum_{s=1}^k w_s d_s}{\sum_{s=1}^k w_s}},
\]
while \textcolor{black}{preserving the privacy of each $d_s$}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The disturbance server generates a random noise vector $\{v_s\}$ and uploads encrypted reciprocals
\[
\textcolor{black}{\mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(v_s^{-1})}
\]
to the blockchain. This enables computation of encrypted ratios
\[
\textcolor{black}{\mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(d_s v_s^{-1}) = \mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(d_s) \otimes \mathrm{Enc}_{pk_s}(v_s^{-1})}
\]
without revealing $d_s$. The data requester samples a random scalar $\textcolor{black}{\mathcal{C}}$ and computes masked weights
\[
\textcolor{black}{m_s = \mathcal{C} w_s v_s}, \quad S_w = \sum_{s=1}^k w_s,
\]
which are aggregated on-chain to obtain
\[
\sum_s m_s \cdot \frac{d_s}{v_s} = \textcolor{black}{\mathcal{C} \sum_s w_s d_s}.
\]
By removing the mask $\textcolor{black}{\mathcal{C}}$, the exact noise-free ETV is obtained:
\[
\textcolor{black}{X = \frac{\sum_s w_s d_s}{\sum_s w_s}}.
\]
All blockchain operations involve only masked or ratio values, ensuring the confidentiality of individual $d_s$.
\end{proof}
\subsection{EWMA Reputation Convergence}
\begin{theorem}[Reputation Convergence]
\label{thm:ewma_convergence}
Let $R_s^{(t)} \in [0,1]$ denote the reputation of user $w^s$ at time $t$, updated according to
\[
\textcolor{black}{R_s^{(t+1)} = R_s^{(t)} + \beta (Z_s^{(t)} - R_s^{(t)})}, \quad \beta \in (0,1),
\]
where $\textcolor{black}{Z_s^{(t)} \in \{0,1\}}$ indicates the validity of reported data. If the long-term expected validity is $\textcolor{black}{\bar{Z}_s = \mathbb{E}[Z_s^{(t)}]}$, then
\[
\lim_{t \to \infty} \textcolor{black}{R_s^{(t)}} = \bar{Z}_s.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Taking expectation on both sides gives
\[
\mathbb{E}[R_s^{(t+1)}] = \mathbb{E}[R_s^{(t)}] + \beta (\bar{Z}_s - \mathbb{E}[R_s^{(t)}]).
\]
Denoting $\textcolor{black}{E_t = \mathbb{E}[R_s^{(t)}]}$, this forms a first-order linear difference equation
\[
E_{t+1} - E_t = \beta (\bar{Z}_s - E_t),
\]
whose solution is
\[
\textcolor{black}{E_t = \bar{Z}_s + (E_0 - \bar{Z}_s) (1-\beta)^t}.
\]
As $t \to \infty$, $(1-\beta)^t \to 0$ for $0<\beta<1$, implying
\[
\lim_{t\to\infty} E_t = \bar{Z}_s.
\]
Thus, the EWMA-based reputation converges to the expected long-term validity of the user's data.
\end{proof}
\subsection{Dynamic Interval Verification Reliability}
\begin{corollary}[Interval Verification Reliability]
\label{cor:interval}
Let $\textcolor{black}{p_s = \Pr(d_s \in [X-\Delta, X+\Delta])}$. Then the long-term expected reputation satisfies
\[
\mathbb{E}[R_s^{(t)}] \to \textcolor{black}{p_s} \quad \text{as } t \to \infty.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
From Theorem~\ref{thm:ewma_convergence}, the EWMA reputation converges to the expected value of $\textcolor{black}{Z_s^{(t)}}$. By definition of the interval verification, this expectation equals $\textcolor{black}{p_s}$, establishing the stated convergence.
\end{proof}
\subsection{Convergence of Weight Iteration}
\begin{lemma}[Weight Iteration Convergence]
\label{lem:weight_convergence}
Let $w_s^{(t)}$ be the weight of user $w^s$ at iteration $t$ for noisy ETV computation. The iterative updates
\[
\textcolor{black}{w_s^{(t+1)} = \frac{\log\left(\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{k}(D_j - X^{(t)})^2}{(D_s - X^{(t)})^2}\right)}{\log\left(\sum_{j=1}^{k}(D_j - X^{(t)})^2\right)}}
\]
converge to a fixed point $\textcolor{black}{w_s^* \propto 1/(D_s - X)^2}$ under the condition that $|X^{(t+1)} - X^{(t)}| < \epsilon$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The weight update is continuous in $X^{(t)}$ and $D_s$, while the ETV is defined as
\[
\textcolor{black}{X^{(t)} = \frac{\sum_s w_s^{(t)} D_s}{\sum_s w_s^{(t)}}}.
\]
This defines a contraction mapping under standard assumptions, ensuring convergence of both $X^{(t)}$ and $w_s^{(t)}$. At the fixed point, the update formula reduces to
\[
\textcolor{black}{w_s^* \propto \frac{1}{(D_s - X)^2}},
\]
confirming the stability of the iterative process within finite steps.
\end{proof}
