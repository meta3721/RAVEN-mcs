Author verdict (honest):
- The Delta_c-s advantage does NOT translate into fewer persistently
  under-served deployment groups. Persistently under-served pair counts are
  method-invariant. RAVEN, FedAvg-Window, and TwoStage-Hajek are identical
  on the 0.5*mu coverage count.
- Versus FedAU/ObsUse the coverage count is Holm-significantly lower for
  RAVEN, but the gap is 5.8/13867.5 (~0.04%) on SensorScope and
  4.65/6684.1 (~0.07%) on U-Air. That is not a material deployment gain.
- This does NOT support the claim that "worse Delta_c-s -> worse group-level
  deployment outcomes" at the coverage-threshold operationalization.
  Delta_c-s remains a statement about mass intensity among already-covered
  pairs, not about which pairs cross a half-share coverage cutoff.
