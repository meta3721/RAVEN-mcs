Author verdict (honest):
- B1 NOT SUPPORTED. Worst-group RMSE does not follow Delta_c-s. On U-Air,
  participation reweighting (FedAU/ObsUse) has the better worst group, matching
  RMSE_mu, not the structural ranking. On SensorScope the methods tie.
- B2 NOT SUPPORTED as a positive structure-to-prediction link. Within a seed,
  methods with worse Delta_c-s tend to have better RMSE_mu on U-Air (FedAU
  pattern) and no relationship on SensorScope.
- VERDICT_B1=NOT_SUPPORTED
- VERDICT_B2=NOT_SUPPORTED
- Paper: replace the "worst-group not recomputed" sentence with this negative
  result. Do not claim a deployment-error gain. Do not use Tail_RMSE.
