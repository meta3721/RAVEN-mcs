Author verdict (honest)
- U-Air fine P2 L1 (144-d) is often geometrically flat along the simplex: ||M v - mu||_1 can be identical for different v. Do not use that as evidence that beta is on-target.
- U-Air coarse-4 ||M beta-mu|| ON-worse frac 20.6%; ||M alpha-mu|| ON-worse frac 2.2%.
- U-Air ||alpha-beta||_1 is larger with Design ON (0.330 vs 0.194). P2 systematically pulls away from the Design reference.
- U-Air clip_rate_stage1 ON 0.0260 vs OFF 0.0001. Stored clip rate is modest; extreme-zeta is not the main persisted signal.
- U-Air seed means: Delta_group ON vs OFF +7.499%; RMSE_mu +7.072%; Delta_c_s -4.753% (positive = Design-on worse).
- RMSE gap remains large. Even if beta is off-target, a P2 constraint alone is not enough to claim the Design problem is solved.
- SensorScope seed means: Delta_group -1.228%; RMSE_mu -0.005%; Delta_c_s -0.595%.
- Support shift is the main U-Air mechanism signal: only 741/1402 active window pairs (52.9%) share the same client set. SensorScope is 1877/1877 (100%). Design changes who is in A_r, not only the weights on a shared support.
- Therefore matched-window P2/coarse comparisons understate the seed-level Delta_group/RMSE reversal. B1 (same-window constrained P2) cannot repair a support change.
