\subsection{The $\kappa=1000$ projection-with-debt rule}
\label{app:kappa_probe}

The projection-with-debt rule is a new rule of this study, not the frozen RAVEN
configuration, and it is reported as exploratory: it sets the debt weight to
$\kappa=1000$ instead of the frozen value, and that weight was chosen on the
tuning seeds.  Against the projection alone it adds $+1.347$ pp and $+0.580$ pp
(Holm $\approx10^{-5}$ on both), on SensorScope and U-Air respectively; at the
frozen debt strength the same contrast is $+0.0073$ pp ($[0.0064,0.0083]$) on
SensorScope, inside the equivalence band of
Section~\ref{subsec:equivalence_band} in the main paper.  Its implementation
gate is not met.  The trained rules are reported as approximate against an exact
projection oracle, measured by $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{impl}}$, the distance
between a trained rule's server weights and the exact minimizer of its
projection problem.  That distance is reported in the $\Delta$ units of
the structural endpoints and not as a relative tolerance: over all $240$ rule
runs $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{impl}}$ is at most $2.42\times10^{-5}$ in those units,
equivalently $1.21\times10^{-3}$ in the percentage-point convention of
Section~\ref{subsec:equivalence_band}.  Against the smallest structural reading
it accompanies, the frozen-strength debt contrast of $+0.0073$ pp
($1.46\times10^{-4}$ in the same units), that is $17\%$; against the two headline
structural contrasts, the design factor at $0.922$ pp and the projection against
uniform weights at $0.768$ pp ($1.84\times10^{-2}$ and $1.54\times10^{-2}$), it is
$0.13\%$ and $0.16\%$.  It is not comparable to
prediction-endpoint percentages and is not reported as such.  Every reading
stays orders of magnitude below the endpoint effect it accompanies, so no sign
or reading changes.  These deltas belong to the new rule, and none of them is a
measurement of RAVEN's performance.
