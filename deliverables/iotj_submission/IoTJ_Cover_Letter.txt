Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider our manuscript, “RAVEN-MCS: Target-Mass Calibration for Windowed Federated Data Completion in Mobile Crowdsensing,” for publication as a regular research article in IEEE Internet of Things Journal.

RAVEN-MCS addresses population distortion caused by sequential sensing and update selection, together with the constraints of finite aggregation windows. It links opportunity, observation, and usable-update correction to convex calibration under client-weight caps and effective-support constraints. This connects normalized local training with server reference masses and executable client weights, coordinating reference fidelity, deployment-group alignment, and accumulated under-service. Under stated assumptions, we establish ideal target-moment recovery and calibration uniqueness, together with reference non-expansion on shared support with otherwise fixed window state.

In two City Scanner protocols with controlled update delivery, RAVEN-MCS achieves the lowest mean group mismatch among five evaluated methods, reducing it by 25.8% and 23.3% relative to TwoStage-Hájek. Matched-support comparisons, ablations, and support-geometry analyses explain the roles and adjustment limits of reconstruction and calibration. Detailed proofs, protocols, and additional results accompany the manuscript as supplementary material.

The study addresses data representativeness and constrained federated aggregation for mobile IoT sensing, making it relevant to the journal’s readership in smart-city and environmental monitoring applications.

All authors have approved this manuscript and its submission to IEEE Internet of Things Journal. The work has not been previously published or publicly presented, has no conference predecessor or other public version, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere.
